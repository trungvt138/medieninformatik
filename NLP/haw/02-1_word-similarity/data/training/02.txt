On Tuesday Mabel arrived before sunrise.
The northern wall stood in the shadow of a tall tree.
A note on the door announced a surprise inspection.
The inspector was a duck named Professor Quack.
Nobody could remember who had hired the duck.
Professor Quack carried a notebook under one wing.
He examined the panels with a stern expression.
Gerald tried to eat the notebook.
The professor moved it to his other wing.
Mabel explained how the glass insulation was made.
The material contained recycled glass from old bottles.
A factory heated the glass until it melted.
The workers turned it into fine soft fibres.
These fibres trapped air inside the insulation.
Trapped air slowed the movement of heat.
The professor wrote a long note about the fibres.
Gerald wrote nothing because he was a goat.
The mayor brought a fresh pot of tea.
The professor requested a cup without bread.
Mabel measured another window with her ruler.
The frame needed a narrow strip of insulation.
She cut a panel to the correct size.
A clean cut made the joint easier to seal.
The professor checked the plane of the wall with his beak.
He nodded twice and dropped his notebook.
Gerald caught it before it hit the ground.
Everyone applauded this unexpected act of kindness.
Gerald then ate the last page.
The professor decided that the page was unnecessary.
At noon a delivery truck blocked the street.
Its driver could not find the town hall.
The mayor pointed at the enormous stone building.
The driver said the building looked like a library.
Mabel unloaded the remaining panels with the team.
They stored the materials under a waterproof cover.
Dark clouds gathered above the square.
The wind lifted one corner of the cover.
Gerald stood on it until the storm passed.
The professor awarded him an imaginary medal.
Gerald tried to eat the imaginary medal.
Mabel finished sealing the window frames.
The inside of the hall was pleasantly warm.
The mayor removed one of his three coats.
The professor removed his serious expression.
The pigeon returned with another red ribbon.
This time it landed on the ladder.
Mabel packed her ruler and her gloves.
The team agreed to meet again on Wednesday.
One final wall still needed their attention.
The mayor promised to bring cake.
