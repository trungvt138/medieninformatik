Wednesday began with cake and an unusual announcement.
The mayor wanted the town hall ready for a dance.
A band would arrive at sunset.
Mabel looked at the final wall and sighed.
Gerald looked at the cake and smiled.
The professor arrived carrying a new notebook.
He had written his name on every page.
Mabel reviewed the plan before starting work.
The final wall contained a large glass door.
A narrow gap ran along the bottom edge.
The team placed a seal beneath the door.
Mabel checked the seal from both sides.
The mayor opened and closed the door twelve times.
He said this was an important scientific test.
The professor wrote down the number twelve.
Gerald counted by eating twelve blades of grass.
The first panel fitted neatly beside the door.
The second panel refused to fit at all.
Mabel discovered a crooked wooden bracket.
She adjusted the bracket with a small tool.
The panel then slid into its proper place.
A loud cheer came from the square.
The band had arrived three hours early.
Its drummer played a very short celebration.
The pigeon responded with an impressive whistle.
The mayor asked the bird to join the band.
Mabel sealed the last joint before lunch.
She inspected every window and every panel.
The professor checked his notes with care.
Gerald checked the remaining cake with equal care.
The walls were ready for the cold weather.
Warm air stayed inside the town hall.
The mayor removed his final winter coat.
He revealed a bright yellow dancing costume.
The professor dropped his notebook in surprise.
Gerald politely returned the notebook uneaten.
At sunset the band played in the hall.
The pigeon sang from the top of a chair.
The mayor danced with remarkable enthusiasm.
Mabel enjoyed a quiet cup of coffee.
Gerald danced with a waterproof cover.
Nobody asked him to explain his technique.
The professor presented Mabel with a gold star.
He presented Gerald with a fresh cabbage.
The mayor thanked the whole team.
Outside the evening air was cold and clear.
Inside the building everyone stayed warm.
Mabel closed the door and smiled.
The town hall was ready for winter.
Gerald ate the last piece of cake.
