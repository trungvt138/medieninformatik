Rules (short)

Story. Two friends are racing through a toy store. One loves collecting different colors of the same toy. The other loves collecting different toys in the same color. Slide toys along clear aisles and place new arrivals to build your collection.

  • Board: 6×6. Tiles: 36 (6 Toys × 6 Colors). Display: 6 tiles.
  • Your turn: (1) Slide exactly 1 toy along its row/column any distance until blocked. (2) Place exactly 1 toy from the display into any empty space. Refill the display.
  • Focus: Each player scores only contiguous (orthogonal) groups that match their focus: Toys or Colors.
  • Points per group size: 2→1, 3→3, 4→6, 5→10, 6→15.
  • End: When the board is full, compute scores.

Tip: Open a lane with your slide, then place to touch multiple clusters at once.
