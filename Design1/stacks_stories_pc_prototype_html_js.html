Rules (short)

Story. Past midnight, the stacks stir. You guide the motion: slide books down clear aisles and shelve new returns to grow your favored collections.

  • Board: 6×6. Tiles: 36 (6 Genres × 6 Jacket Colors). Display: 6 tiles.
  • Your turn: (1) Slide exactly 1 book along its row/column any distance until blocked. (2) Shelve exactly 1 book from the display into any empty space. Refill the display.
  • Focus: Each player scores only contiguous (orthogonal) groups that match their Genres or Colors focus.
  • Points per group size: 2→1, 3→3, 4→6, 5→10, 6→15.
  • End: When the board is full, compute scores.

Tip: Slide to open a lane, then shelve to touch multiple clusters at once.
